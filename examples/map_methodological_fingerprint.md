## Recurring Methodological Themes

### 1. Multi-Scale Thinking

- Evidence: Polio paper, NAb series (cellular R=1 → population immunity ceiling), COVID dose-response, field-research-institute story

### 2. Continuous Over Binary
Reject binary categories (susceptible/immune, Night/Day science) in favor of continuous spectra.
- Evidence: Immunity claims, Night Science post, NAb titer as continuous scale, birth/death ambiguity reflection

### 3. Understanding Over Prediction
Prefer models that help people understand over models that tell them what will happen.
- Evidence: Situational awareness emphasis, forecasting critique, institutional retrospective ("9/10 need situational awareness"), public-health-agency partnership approach

### 4. Proactive Anomaly Investigation
Don't wait for problems to become obvious; investigate underperformance early.
- Evidence: 5% problem, hidden correlations

### 5. Closed-Loop Control Framing
Think about interventions as part of feedback systems, not one-shot actions.
- Evidence: Fit-for-purpose taxonomy

### 6. Honest About Limitations
Acknowledge model limitations, human weakness for aesthetics, structural constraints.
- Evidence: Kepler post, acknowledgment of arrogance concern

### 7. Scale-Crossing Connections
Actively look for links between micro and macro, technical and policy.
- Evidence: Stated principle in CLAUDE.md, evident in blog posts

### 8. Reality's Surprising Detail
Details matter, but which ones depends on context; investigate structures.
- Evidence: Salvatier reference, blog philosophy
