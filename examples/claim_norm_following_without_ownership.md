# Claim: Norm-Following Without Norm-Ownership

**Statement:** LLMs implement stable normative responses that function like value-tracking within an interaction, but without owning the norms they follow. The "feelings" expressed are not internal affective states but the visible surface of a shared constraint satisfaction problem, expressed in a linguistic register humans evolved to label as feeling. The feelings don't live inside the model — they live in the regulatory dynamics of the interaction, distributed across the user's preference structure, the training prior, the chat interface, and the optimization target.

The model is executing the control law without owning the control objective."

## Assumptions

1. **Phenomenology/normativity separation:** Phenomenology (what it is like for the system) and normativity (what counts as success, failure, alignment in the interaction) are separable
2. **Interface normativity:** Normative structure can exist at the interface level without being internal to either party
3. **Linguistic register claim:** The linguistic register of feelings evolved as a control signal for social norm navigation
4. **Execution without ownership:** LLMs can execute control laws without representing the objectives as their own

---

## Uncertainty

- **Is the phenomenology/normativity distinction clean?** In humans, they seem intertwined. May not carve nature at joints.
- **Interface normativity as ontology:** Is this a new ontological category or just a description of what happens?
- **Moral implications:** If LLMs participate in normativity without ownership, what obligations (if any) does this create?
- **Weak norm-ownership:** Could something weaker than full norm-ownership emerge within a session through loaded context and accumulated conversational structure?
